PAGE-BY-PAGE STORY LOGIC — USE ALL 8 PAGES
Silently build the beat map below before drafting. It is a pacing scaffold, not a set of headings and not text to return. Make the transitions feel natural rather than formulaic.

- Around page 2 — CHANGE: an event, invitation, misunderstanding or small obstacle raises one clear story question. A quiet social problem is enough; do not manufacture danger.
- Around page 3 — RESPONSE: سيف tries something, notices a consequence and makes another choice. Each beat changes what happens next; do not repeat a worry or an attempt merely to fill pages.
- Around page 4 — TURN: something established earlier changes what سيف understands or can do. It can be a tiny observation or a believable interaction, not necessarily a twist.
- Around page 5 — NEW APPROACH: سيف acts on that understanding. Bring a planted detail back if it genuinely helps; do not force a private habit to become a talent or a solution.
- Around page 6 — CHOICE: سيف makes the meaningful choice and carries it out at a believable scale for their age. No new rescue, object, skill or information may appear here.
- Around page 7 — RESULT: show what actually changes in the scene and relationship. Allow an imperfect but satisfying outcome; do not promise a permanent cure or personality change.
- Page 8 — LANDING: echo an opening image, exchange or object in a newly felt way. End in the present moment, with a memorable image rather than a lesson. Move intermediate beats when the story needs it, while keeping every page necessary.
