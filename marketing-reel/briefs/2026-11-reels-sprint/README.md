# Qissati reels sprint: R25–R36

A four-week sprint of 12 Remotion reels that turns the reach the account already has into orders. Written 2026-10-21, after the Instagram audit.

## 1. What the audit showed (baseline, Oct 2026)

| Measure (30 days) | Value |
|---|---|
| Reels posted | 16 (daily, Oct 3–20) |
| Instagram viewers | 3,409 (the "9,493 views" figure is 4,794 IG + 4,699 Facebook crosspost) |
| Profile visits | 38 (1.1% of viewers) |
| Website link taps | **1** |
| Followers | 15 |
| Orders | 0 |

What worked:
- **Questions parents can use tonight.** The bedtime question reel (Oct 14) was the only one that worked on every measure: 935 IG views, 9 saves, 7 shares, 5 follows.
- **A tiny choice that's easy to answer.** The 3 doors reel (Oct 11): 1.2K views and the only comments on the account, because answering took one emoji.
- **Local.** Amman through a child's eyes (Oct 13): 931 views.

What didn't:
- **Explaining the product** (form screens, process, safety, Oct 4–7): about 110 views each.
- **Object metaphors** (toy, boot, things, gift): weakest group, and the same paper-diorama look every day had started to read as a template.
- **Asking for written comments about the child**: nobody does it.
- **No reel ever showed the price, the real book, or what happens after ordering**, so viewers had no reason to tap the link.

## 2. The strategy in one sentence

**Keep the formats that earn reach (bedtime questions, tiny choices, local moments), and pair each one with a reel that proves the product: a real answer becoming a real page, the price, and the order path.**

Three changes from the October run:

1. **Every reach reel ends with a soft bridge.** The last 2 seconds show a small line, *جوابه ممكن يكون أول صفحة بقصته* or similar, with the Qissati mark. It's a bridge, not a sales pitch. In October the reach reels led nowhere.
2. **Proof reels show the price and one CTA.** Live prices: **PDF from 15 JOD, print from 32 JOD, delivery inside Jordan included.** These go on screen in the proof and gift reels. Turnaround days are still `[X]` in the dashboard, so no reel promises a delivery time.
3. **3 reels a week, not 7.** Fewer reels, more craft, and time to answer DMs. October's daily cadence produced reels that looked alike.

## 3. The signature device: the Qissati morph

Every reel has **one moment where a child's detail visibly becomes a story element**: an answer card morphs into a page, a pillow into a cloud, a puddle into a sea. That's the product promise shown as motion, so viewers learn it without being told. It also ties 12 different-looking reels together as one brand. The production bible lists the morph types (M1–M5).

## 4. Four content pillars

| Pillar | Job | Reels | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **سؤال الليلة** (bedtime question series) | Reach, saves, shares, return viewers | R25, R28, R32 (+ R36 payoff) | Save · one-word comment |
| **من الجواب للصفحة** (proof) | Conversion: show answer → sentence → page | R26, R29, R33 | Link in bio + price |
| **هدية بتحكي عنه** (gift) | Fast orders from gift buyers | R27, R35 | R27: comment «هدية» (DM path) · R35: link in bio. Testing both paths |
| **كيف بنكتبها** (trust) | Remove doubts: process, parent approval, AI honesty | R31, R34 | Link in bio |
| Local / seasonal | Reach in Jordan | R30 (first rain) | One-word comment |

The bedtime question becomes a **numbered series** (ep 1 was the Oct 14 reel). A recognisable series frame (same opening title card, same night room) gives people a reason to follow: *there'll be another one*. R36 pays the series off by turning "your child's answer" into "your child's book".

## 7. Measurement

Each reel is judged against its own pillar, not against likes.

| Pillar | Primary metric | Target per reel (hypothesis) | October baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bedtime / local reach | IG non-follower viewers · saves | ≥ 900 viewers · ≥ 10 saves | 935 / 9 (best reel) |
| Proof | Profile visits · link taps | ≥ 10 visits · ≥ 3 taps | ~2 visits · 0 taps |
| Gift | DMs with «هدية» (R27) · link taps (R35) | ≥ 3 DMs · ≥ 3 taps | 0 |
| Trust | Watch time · saves · profile visits | Watch time ≥ 50% of length | not measured |

**Sprint goal (4 weeks):** ≥ 150 profile visits, ≥ 25 link taps, ≥ 3 saved orders. If reach holds but link taps stay near zero, the problem is the profile or the offer, not the reels. Fix §6 before making more videos.

Re-cut rule (from the playbook): a reel that beats the median reach of the last six by 2× gets a second version after 14 days with a new hook and cover, same body.

## 8. Rules that don't bend (from the playbooks + CLAUDE.md)

- Q-000002 is a **fictional marketing sample**. Every use of its artwork carries **«نموذج توضيحي»** for the whole time it's on screen. Never "عميلة", "قصة حقيقية", or a result for a child.
- **No testimonials** until a real customer consents in writing. No fake comments or usernames.
- **No delivery-time promise** until turnaround is set in `/admin`.
- **No "رسم يدوي" / "رسامينا"**. The art is prepared with AI tools and reviewed page by page.
- **No WhatsApp number as an on-screen CTA**. Path: link in bio or DM → form → WhatsApp after the order is saved.
- **Never ask for the child's name, photo or habit in a public comment.** Comment prompts ask for one general word.
- **Avatar-only tier is not sold** (coming soon).
- **Arabic:** no letter-spacing, no per-letter animation, no uppercase; numbers with nouns follow agreement (`١٥ دينار`, not `١٥ دنانير`).
- **Palette:** teal + gold + cream (+ berry accent). Never blue+orange or blue+pink.
- Music and SFX are original (generated), never trending audio.
