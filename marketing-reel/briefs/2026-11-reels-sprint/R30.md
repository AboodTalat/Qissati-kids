> Create **Qissati-R30** (20 s, 600 frames) and **Qissati-R30-Cover** from `briefs/2026-11-reels-sprint/R30.md`. Scene: an original stylised Amman hillside in paper-craft (you may reuse and re-light the warm-stone blocks and stairs from `src/r16/`; no photoreal landmarks, no maps, no people). Re-light it for the first rain of winter: cooler teal-grey paper, low-contrast rain streaks (≤ 25% opacity, slow, never strobing), wet highlights in cream, warm gold window lights. The camera starts high (C4) and cranes down to child-eye level on a stair landing. A puddle on the landing morphs (M3) into a small paper sea with gentle waves; the stairs fold into a flowing paper river of layered ribbons (M5) that runs downhill; a small berry-red rain boot standing on a step morphs (M2) into a paper boat that sails down the stair-river. The camera follows the boat in one slow dolly, then rises a little to frame the question. No child appears; the boot is the child's presence. Music: cosy original theme (felt piano + soft rain-stick texture), ducked under voice. SFX: gentle rain bed, a single drip on the morph, water lap, a soft paper fold.
